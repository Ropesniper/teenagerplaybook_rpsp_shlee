Understanding Behavioral Fixation

Behavioral fixation refers to the tendency to repeatedly rely on familiar patterns of thinking or behavior, even when alternative approaches may be more effective. Teenager Playbook explores how adolescents can recognize, analyze, and gradually reshape such patterns through structured learning and reflective practice.
